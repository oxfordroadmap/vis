Microsoft 2025 Geopolitical Views: Kevin Scott’s `Agentic Web`
 
What if this is **the** moment—like the '90s all over again—when anyone can build without asking, move fast without fences, and ship code that delivers?

Microsoft's CTO certainly thinks so. In the talk [The `Agentic Web`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmHJFRNHmoc&t=1385s), [Kevin Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Scott_\(computer_scientist\)) lays out a bold vision for where the internet might be headed: not just a more powerful web, but a more **agentic** one—where intelligent digital agents fundamentally change how we interact, build, and even govern online spaces.

<!--more-->

## 🗺️ Context

In a May 2025 keynote aimed at developers, Kevin Scott shared his insights and vision on recent AI advancements under the term **`Agentic Web`**. 

Kevin Scott is a computer scientist, currently the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Microsoft.

He also highlighted the ideological dimensions of the Web as the most exciting time since the 90s where the Web began.  

This post summarizes the his insights on the `Agentic Web`, with ideological dimensions highlighted and geopolitical implications discussed.

## 🌐 The `Agentic Web`

At around **00:23:05**, Keven Scott dives into the **value core** of this vision:

### ⚙️ Value Core

- ✨ **Agent-Centric Design**: Your agent becomes your proxy—aligned with your goals, values, and ethics. No longer a passive browser, you become an ***empowered actor*** in a more ***interactive web***.    
- 🗫 **Pluralism over Monoculture**: Rather than a one-size-fits-all tech stack, the `Agentic Web` encourages ***multiple agent ecosystems***, each reflecting different ***cultural and philosophical viewpoints***.    
- 🛡️ **Ethical Entitlements**: Agents should ***preserve*** user memory, ***enforce***data boundaries, and ***adapt*** behavior based on your personal needs—***not*** the platform’s incentives.

But Kevin Scott doesn’t stop at ideology. 

### 🌍 Ideological and Geopolitical Implications

He pulls back the curtain on the **ideological and geopolitical implications**:

- 🧠 **AI Memory as Infrastructure**: Systems like RAG aren’t just tech tricks—they’re ***strategic assets***. Nations may treat agent architectures the way they treat semiconductors or power grids.
    
- 🏢 **Decentralization vs. Platform Monopoly**: Will this new wave of intelligent tools ***flourish independently***, or be ***captured by mega-platforms***? That tension could shape the future of innovation.
    
- 🎏 **Cultural Stakes**: Countries are already racing to define the ***norms around agent design***—and whoever controls that narrative could influence how digital society evolves.

### 🕊️ High-level thinking
In Microsoft's 2025 CTO keynote, we witness the intersection of **software design, AI architecture, and global politics**.

Kevin Scott, a computer scientist born in 1972 who grew up with the earliest Web, notes the distinct ideological and geopolitical shifts today, while echoing the excitement he felt in his 20s when the Web first emerged. (I can personally relate to this.)

Microsoft's CTO clearly conveys that this moment isn’t just about building better tools—it’s about deciding who shapes the future internet and under what values.
