### C. 🎯 Education as a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

This curriculum embraces “Education as a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)”—prioritizing essential knowledge that enables learners to perform confidently and responsibly in real-world roles. Instead of memorizing fragmented facts (**know-whats**), students focus on **know-whys**—deeper understanding that fuels **know-hows**, or applied professional practices.

---

#### 🧭 Mechanisms of Know-Whys

To help learners internalize skills through iterative practice and self-awareness, the following structures scaffold deeper conceptual learning:

- 🎯 **Capstone Projects** – Final portfolios act as culminating artifacts of project-based learning, synthesizing technical and sociotechnical knowledge
- 👥 **Role-Based Collaboration** – Four career-aligned threads simulate interdisciplinary teamwork and reflect how work is divided and recombined across product lifecycles
- 🧠 **Progressive Learning Cycles** – Drafting real-world documents (MRDs, PRDs, UX maps) becomes a metacognitive toolkit for handling ambiguity, surfacing intent, and converting vision into action—from "Heroes" (who fix) to "Agents" (who shape)

---

#### 🛠️ Mechanisms of Know-Hows (Through Professional Roles)

Skill development happens through immersive role play, where learners adopt the responsibilities and decision-making processes of professionals:

- 📌 Project Managers draft briefs and roadmaps
- 🧩 Designers prototype interfaces and document visual logic
- 🔍 Researchers chart user journeys and unpack systemic value

All anchored in collaborative production and cumulative reflection.

---

#### 📦 Learning Outcomes: Personal Portfolios as MVPs

Portfolios are the centerpiece of learner growth—both a record of achievement and a living demonstration of project-based mastery:

- 💼 Learners deliver final portfolios as curated sets of MVPs—each showcasing applied understanding through tangible outputs
- 🧑‍💻 Learners express fluency in industry-relevant roles (PM, UI/Frontend, UX research/design) via domain-specific formats and documentation
- 🔗 Learners collaborate across threads, contributing artifacts such as MRDs, BRDs, PRDs; dashboards, app prototypes, UI kits; user research reports, market briefs, technical specs, and reflective essays
