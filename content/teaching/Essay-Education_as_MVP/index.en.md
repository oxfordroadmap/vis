## 🤔Education as MVP Philosophy

The curriculum reimagines university learning by adopting the concept of **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**, in response to the fast-paced, outcome-driven demands of the Internet industry.

The curriculum translates key practices from the Internet industry—especially **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** and **Continuous Integration & Deployment (CI/CD)**—into a structured educational philosophy. Grounded with modern learning frameworks like **Project-Based Learning (PBL)** and **Outcomes-Based Learning (OBL)**, this integration fosters agile, career-relevant education for future digital creators.


### 🧩 Key Terms
* 🚀`Minimum Viable Product` (MVP) – Originating from software development, MVP emphasizes _time-to-value_ and [validated learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Validated_learning). Students prototype early, iterate often, and refine through continuous feedback—just as products are tested and evolved in tech startups.
* 🔁 `CI/CD mindset` – Borrowed directly from software engineering, this mindset drives students to constantly improve their outputs—versioning their work, curating modular content, and publishing on platforms like GitHub for real-world visibility and relevance.
* 📈`Outcomes-based Assessment` (OBL) – Students document and reflect on their progress, using peer and self-assessment to align deliverables with professional benchmarks and community standards.
* 🧪`Project-based Learning` (PBL) – Every module leads to real-world prototypes. Final projects are deployed artifacts—live, shareable, and skill-representative.

### 📐Formula
`Education as MVP = OBL + PBL + CI/CD`

### 🔍 Teaching Philosophy Reframed
MVP brings clarity and purpose to teaching: rather than lecturing static content, educators guide learners through **cycles of iteration**, **feedback**, and **deployment**. Teaching becomes facilitation of authentic product creation—not abstract memorization.

Instructors model industry workflows, helping students cultivate cross-functional skills through collaborative prototyping and portfolio curation.

### 🦋 Learning Experience Transformed
With PBL at the core, students become makers—designing, debugging, and deploying from day one. They engage in hands-on, interdisciplinary projects that build both skill and identity.

Every **time-to-impact** artifact—from product briefs to web interfaces—serves as proof of learning and readiness. Graduates walk away not just with concepts, but with _shipping-ready portfolios_ aligned to Internet-era job roles: Web Developers, UI/UX Designers, Product Managers, and API-integrated creators.

### 🧠 In Summary

By aligning education with MVP and CI/CD practices, the curriculum reflects the Internet industry's focus on speed, relevance, and adaptability. It fosters a _coherent teaching-learning ecosystem_ that is agile, purpose-driven, and deeply responsive to the convergence of learning and digital product creation. Students engage in authentic, career-aligned projects that emphasize **autonomy**, **iteration**, and **interdisciplinary practice**, with openly accessible portfolios that showcase the skills they’ve developed.
