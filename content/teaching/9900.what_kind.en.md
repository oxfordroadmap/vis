## What kinds ❓

This teaching and training documentation focuses on:

* AI Agent application product innovations
* AI Agent application product development
* Writing (professionally) for the above
* Writing/Mapping Innovations/Scientific Frontiers (professionally) for the above
* Writing the geopolitical implications for the above

By design, the documentations are open to the adoption of:

* any disciplines
* any industry sectors
* any knowledge domains

### Terms of Focus
Focusing on **product**, **user experience (UX/AX)**, and **geopolitics**, this training materials documented and curated the most relevant and useful content regarding:
- 🧪 Product strategy, design, and shipping
- 🧭 UX/AX (Agent Experience, interface design, user-agent collaboration)
- 🌍 Geopolitical implications of AI agent deployment, infrastructure, and regulation

## Selection Bias
The author acknowledges the following selection bias:
* A strong bias towards cross-disciplinary **practical usefulness** and **critical reflections**. Thus, the application domains can vary.  
* A strong bias towards critical reflections on the notion of ***"global commons"*** that features a frame (or the point of origin) starts from **open science**, **open data**, and **open knowledge**.
* A strong bias towards analytical approach towards **value alignment** and **value propositions** in discussing the above topics, especially with the digital notions of innovations, markets, democracy, authoritarianism and dictatorship.
* A strong bias towards the author's life experience as a Taiwanese citizen who was trained in Taiwanese, UK and European institutions, and worked in the universities in China, the United States, Europe and the United Nations University (a think tank).
* A strong bias towards the AI alignment with human development (including the Sustainable Development Goals)
