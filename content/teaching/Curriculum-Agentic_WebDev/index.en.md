## ⚡TLDR

The `{{% param "emoji" %}}{{% param "curriculum" %}}` curriculum guides learners through the frontier of AI-integrated web systems, emphasizing agentic design, multilingual adaptability, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) logic. Through hands-on modules and impact-driven projects, students learn not only how to build web applications—but how to design intelligence, model decision contexts, and anticipate futures within ethical, participatory, and data-responsive frameworks.

📚 The curriculum unfolds in **3 progressive stages** across **5+2 modules**, adaptable to semester-based or self-paced formats:

- 🧭 **Stage 1: Foundation** — _AG101 to AG102_: Students master web fundamentals, deploy agentic features using APIs, and integrate LLMs via platforms like 🤗 Hugging Face and 🐙 GitHub.
    
- 🔁 **Stage 2: Production** — _AG201 to AG203_: Structured communication, RAG systems, and agentic data dashboards empower learners to create reactive, multilingual, and interpretive applications.
    
- 🚀 **Stage 3: Capstone Impact** — _AG303 and AG302_: Students synthesize their work into public-facing portfolios, conduct science mapping, and articulate AI impact using forecasting and responsible innovation tools.
    

### ✨ Highlights

- 🔧 **Build** agentic web apps integrating LLMs via 🤗 Hugging Face and 🐙 GitHub for real-world functionality
    
- 📦 **Deliver and deploy** structured expressive intelligence through multilingual content, structured data, PRDs, and agentic workflows
    
- 🔗 **Synthesize and forecast** data, decisions, and storytelling into ethical, adaptive dashboards for public-facing engagement—including code sharing and science mapping
    

### 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- 🖌️ **Construct** structured web applications and interactive LLM-powered systems using modern web frameworks
    
- 📑 **Compose** AI agent product requirements documents (PRDs) structured for code-generation and aligned with professional standards, including W3C accessibility and sustainable web design principles
    
- 🧰 **Communicate** project decisions and value propositions across relevant ecosystems and Communities of Practice (CoPs)
    
- 🎨 **Design** data visualizations and agentic interfaces that respond to user context and ethical considerations
    
- 🧪 **Evaluate** innovation scenarios and impact through AI assessment methods, science mapping, responsible AI frameworks, and foresight methodologies

## 🔖 Curriculum Details

Progressively enriching learners' portfolios through project documentation and interpretive outcomes, this curriculum guides students through a three-stage learning journey. Each stage deepens their technical fluency, innovative imagination, agentic design capacity, and ethical considerations—***regardless of prior programming experience***. Learners begin by mastering Web fundamentals, then evolve agentic interfaces, and finally synthesize innovations with societal impact.

### 🧭 Stage 1: Foundation — _AG101 to AG102_

Stage 1 features API-based Web Development and foundational LLM integration. Students create modular Web architectures and begin shaping user experience with reactive AI tools.

#### 🐙 **AG101: Web Development with AI**

- 🧠 **Essential Web Skills**: Students explore Web APIs and core web design principles—learning API/HTTP/HTML/CSS/JS basics aided by AI code assistants.    
- 🧩 **Modular Design**: Emphasis is placed on responsive, multilingual, and component-based site architecture using Hugo, TailwindCSS, and GitHub workflows. 
- 🐙 **GitHub Versioning & Collaboration**: Students use GitHub for version control, project documentation, and sharing modular templates that evolve into public-facing portfolios.
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Students deliver personal portfolio websites—functional, multilingual, and scalable—marking their entry into web-based agentic creation.
    

#### 🤗 **AG102: Agentic Web Development**

- 🛠️ **LLM API Integration**: Students apply API-based LLMs both locally and in the cloud, experimenting with three scaffolding options: (1) Public Website + Chatbot, (2) Private Smart Notebook + Local LLM, and (3) YouTube Curation Site + Automation.    
- 🔄 **Agentic Web Features**: Projects explore Web components that are reactive, proactive, and iterative—shaping flows that adapt to user needs and ethical considerations.
- 🤗 **Hugging Face Deployment**: Students explore Hugging Face’s model hub and Inference API to deploy language models and integrate custom workflows for agentic functionality.
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Students develop advanced applications that reflect increased structure and interactivity. They begin to interpret how agentic design impacts user experience and decision-making.
    

### 🔁 Stage 2: Production — _AG201, AG202, AG203_

Building upon foundational LLM integrations, this stage focuses on agentic capabilities of AI systems. Students consolidate core skills across three thematic strands: **Structured Communication**, **RAG Systems**, and **Agentic Data Visualization**, culminating in applied and interpretive outputs that highlight human-AI collaboration.

#### ♞🧱 **AG201: Agentic Structured Communication**

- 🧠 **Structured Expression**: Students develop expressive inputs and outputs through structured LLM model specs, markdown graphs, product requirement documents (PRDs), interactive storytelling, and multilingual frameworks.
- 🤝 **Community of Practice (CoP)**: They share experimentations and outcomes across technical ecosystems and sector-facing CoPs—highlighting design choices, contextual nuances, and communicative clarity.
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Outputs emphasize UX clarity and cultural adaptability, showcased through personal portfolio sites. Projects focus on `context engineering` and `prompt engineering` to reflect agentic system design.

#### ♝🍞 **AG202: RAG Systems**

- 🔗 **Data Structure & Retrieval**: Students design Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems to compare structured vs. unstructured data flows, and explore classic vs. graph-based RAG architectures.
- 🤝 **Community of Practice (CoP)**: Work is contextualized through CoP engagement, illuminating how RAG ecosystems differ across journalism, civic tech, and academic sectors.
- ⚙️ **Backend–Frontend Integration**: Students build both the frontend (search and chatbot functionalities) and backend (knowledge corpora architecture and workflow orchestration).
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Completed RAG applications demonstrate dynamic, context-aware interactions—revealing how pipeline orchestration enables adaptive intelligence.

#### ♛🖼️ **AG203: Agentic Data Visualization**

- 👁️‍🗨️ **Decision touchpoints**: Students develop interactive data dashboards that visualize not only data, but **decision contexts and interpretive frames**, epistemic uncertainty, and narrative framing.
- 🧩 **Human–AI Workflows**: Visualization projects interrogate how humans and agents co-participate in decision-making pipelines, emphasizing Human-in-the-Loop and data-driven decision principles.
- 🧬 **Symbolic & Epistemic Structures**: Students explore narrative agency, epistemic modeling, and cultural symbolism as they apply visual languages to interpretive frames.
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Final dashboards are presented as agentic systems—tools that empower users to question, co-author, and operationalize data in ethically reflective ways.

### 🚀 Stage 3: Capstone Impact — _AG303 and AG302_

The final stage centers on synthesis, professional/public engagement, technological forecasting and societal foresight. Students activate innovation mindsets and consolidate reflective documentation through agentic applications, responsible forecasting, and multilingual outreach. The emphasis is on linking learning outcomes to instances of **Community of Practice (CoP)** and articulating impact through structured rubrics and peer walkthroughs.


#### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧬 **AG303: Capstone Project**

- 🧵 **Portfolio Integration**: Students assemble prior learnings into tangible, public-facing final portfolios summarizing all projects and documentation relevant to their learning outcomes.
- 📬 **Professional & Multilingual Outreach**: Capstone outputs emphasize communication strategies and resource deployment tailored for specific CoPs, drawing on professional engagement.    
- 🔗 **Open Source Engagement**: Portfolios are polished with meaningful Git practices—linking repositories, issuing pull requests, and mentoring contributions across ecosystems.    
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Final projects are presented in peer walkthroughs and showcased with public-facing visibility, emphasizing UX clarity, accessibility, and CoP relevance.
    
#### 📚🦠 **AG302: Science Mapping and AI Impact**

- 🗺️ **Technological Foresight**: Students engage with science mapping using patent and/or scientific datasets to evaluate trends and uncover innovation trajectories.    
- 🔮 **Responsible Innovation Frameworks**: Inquiry centers on ethical design choices and societal impact, integrating frameworks of responsible innovation and anticipatory governance.    
- 📊 **Structured Rubrics**: Final evaluations are scaffolded by rubrics that foreground sustainability, cross-cultural communication, and epistemic transparency.    
- 📦 **Delivery & Demonstration**: Insights and projections are communicated through interpretive models and scenario forecasting, contextualizing successes and failures of both CoPs and individual contributions.
